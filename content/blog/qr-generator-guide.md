Make a QR code whose destination can be understood and checked before printing.

Use the [QR Code Generator](/tools/everyday/qr-generator/) alongside this illustrative example.

## A practical example

For a printed event flyer, use the final HTTPS page rather than an editing link or a temporary preview. Scan a trial print on another phone and compare the resulting domain and path with the intended address. Keep a readable text URL alongside the code so the flyer remains useful when scanning fails.

## Check the result

Preserve a clear margin and test at the final print size. A code that scans on a large screen may not scan on textured paper or after a logo covers part of it.

## Try a comparison

Print two trial sizes on the material you will use. Scan from a realistic distance and angle rather than holding the phone perfectly square at close range. Choose a size that works reliably in that context and retain the quiet border.

## What this does not establish

Encoding a URL does not verify the destination’s safety or keep the destination available. Use a page you control and maintain.
