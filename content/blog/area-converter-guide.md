Convert a floor area without accidentally using a length conversion factor.

Use the [Area Converter](/tools/everyday/area-converter/) alongside this illustrative example.

## A practical example

A room measuring 4 m by 3 m covers 12 m². Since one square foot is 0.09290304 m², that area is approximately 129.17 ft². Converting each side and multiplying gives the same area; multiplying 12 by the metres-to-feet factor only once does not.

## Check the result

Compare the room dimensions with the converted area. Keep extra precision through the calculation, then round the final figure to the precision justified by your measurements.

## Try a comparison

Double one side of the example room from 4 m to 8 m while keeping the other side at 3 m. The area doubles to 24 m². Doubling both sides would quadruple it, which matters when scaling a floor plan.

## What this does not establish

An area conversion does not include cutting waste, wall thickness or irregular sections. Measure those separately before ordering flooring.
