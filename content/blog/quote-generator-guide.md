## Step-by-step workflow

1. Open the [Quote & Estimate](/tools/business/quote-generator/) and state the main purpose of the output before entering details.
2. Add the prepared client identity, quote number and date, itemised work details in a consistent order. Keep names, dates, currencies and units aligned.
3. Generate the first quote & estimate result, then compare its most important output with the original source.
4. Change **client identity** once to see whether the conclusion is stable or needs a second scenario.
5. Read the result from the recipient's perspective and remove placeholders, unsupported claims or fields that do not apply.
6. Save only the reviewed version together with the assumptions that explain how it was produced.

## Worked example

A freelance designer quotes a €1,200 website project: €300 discovery, €700 design and €200 handover. The quote states that it is valid for 14 days and that revisions beyond the included two rounds are billed separately.

This Quote & Estimate example is deliberately small enough to audit. Add real-world complexity one item at a time and preserve a baseline; otherwise a change in **client identity** can be confused with a change in **payment terms**.

## What can change the result

The answer is especially sensitive to **client identity** and **quote number and date**. Test both before relying on it. The final input—**payment terms**—may look secondary, but it can change how another person interprets the output even if the headline figure or layout stays the same.

## How to interpret the output

A quote is an offer, not proof of payment. Keep assumptions beside the price so the client can see what would change the total.

Read this quote & estimate output as evidence for the decision to turn a scope and price into a clear pre-work estimate. Check whether it answers that purpose, whether its important statements trace to a source, and whether a reviewer can see what is included and excluded without a separate explanation.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- **Leaving the scope vague.** Correct it before the output becomes a shared record.
- **Omitting an expiry date.** Correct it before the output becomes a shared record.
- **Mixing optional and required work.** Correct it before the output becomes a shared record.
- **Sending a quote with no acceptance path.** Correct it before the output becomes a shared record.

With Quote & Estimate, clean formatting improves comprehension but does not validate **client identity** or the final conclusion. Resolve the listed mistakes before this output becomes a client document, published page or permanent record.

## Quality-control checklist

- Verify the Quote & Estimate result independently against its most important source.
- Confirm **client identity**, **quote number and date**, dates, currencies and units.
- Remove example text and every field that does not apply to this workflow.
- Check the exported result on a phone as well as a desktop.
- Test each link, QR code or call to action in the final version.
- Keep a dated source copy so later edits cannot silently replace the approved result.

### Can I use the first Quote & Estimate result immediately?

Use it as a draft. Recheck **client identity**, test the important calculation, document or link, and expose assumptions a recipient could not otherwise see. High-stakes uses may require a qualified professional.

### Does Vootkit verify my client identity?

No. Quote & Estimate applies the structure or calculation described on its page. Accuracy depends on the supplied inputs and whether this model fits the real situation.

### What should I save with the final output?

Keep the date, version, client identity, quote number and date, important assumptions and source records. That audit trail helps stop an old quote & estimate result being reused after the facts change.
