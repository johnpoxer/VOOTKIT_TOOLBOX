Compare pay using the hours and weeks you will actually be paid for.

Use the [Hourly Wage Calculator](/tools/tax/hourly-wage/) alongside this illustrative example.

## A practical example

At 20 per hour for 30 hours a week over 48 paid weeks, annual gross pay is 28,800. Assuming 52 weeks instead produces 31,200, a 2,400 difference. That difference is an assumption about paid time, not a change in the hourly rate.

## Check the result

Compare both roles on the same basis and list paid leave, overtime and benefits separately. A higher contractor hourly rate may need to cover unpaid administration, time between contracts and business costs.

## Try a comparison

Keep the hourly rate at 20 and increase weekly hours from thirty to thirty-five over forty-eight paid weeks. Gross rises to 33,600. Compare the additional 4,800 with the additional hours required, rather than judging the annual totals alone.

## What this does not establish

Gross hourly conversion does not estimate take-home pay or establish compliance with wage laws. Check the contract and pay periods before budgeting.
