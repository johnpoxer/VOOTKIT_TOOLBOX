Prepare distinct options and an agreed selection rule before making a group choice.

Use the [Random Picker](/tools/everyday/random-picker/) alongside this illustrative example.

## A practical example

For choosing two different presentation topics from six options, request two unique picks. For assigning an independent warm-up question each day, repeats may be intentional. The repeat setting changes the meaning of the selection rather than merely how the output looks.

## Check the result

Give each option a distinct label and remove unintended duplicates. If two people share a name, use a harmless distinguishing label rather than collapsing them into one entry.

## Try a comparison

For a group decision, show the prepared options before making the pick. Agree whether an ineligible result will be removed and redrawn before drawing, not afterward. This makes the process explainable without claiming that a particular random outcome is best.

## What this does not establish

The picker cannot decide whether options are eligible or equally appropriate. Preserve the list and rule if other people need to understand how the choice was made.
