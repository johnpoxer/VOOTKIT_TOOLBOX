Design alert graphics that can be integrated without pretending they deliver events themselves.

Use the [Stream Alert Creator](/tools/video/stream-alert-creator/) alongside this illustrative example.

## A practical example

Create visually distinct assets for a follow and a donation, then load them into the alert service you use. Trigger the service’s own test event while making a private recording. Confirm that the asset appears at a readable size and does not cover the subject or important interface controls.

## Check the result

Check transparency and sound levels in the receiving service, not only in the VOOTKIT preview. Event timing and display duration belong to the integration that plays the graphic.

## Try a comparison

Test each alert type with the provider’s preview controls rather than assuming one successful follow alert proves donations also work. Check placement against the actual camera and content sources. Separate the graphic design check from the event-delivery integration check.

## What this does not establish

An exported design does not connect your channel, collect payments or verify a donor. Keep those functions within the account and provider responsible for them.
