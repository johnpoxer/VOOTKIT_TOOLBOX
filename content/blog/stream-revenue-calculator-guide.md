Reconcile estimated revenue with actual net payouts rather than headline subscription prices.

Use the [Stream Revenue Calculator](/tools/video/stream-revenue-calculator/) alongside this illustrative example.

## A practical example

For an illustrative USD month, 100 subscribers at a net 2.00 each contribute 200. Adding 1,000 Bits at the model’s fixed 0.01 each gives 10; donations of 30 and ad revenue of 20 bring the model total to 260. Use actual dashboard net subscription figures rather than an assumed revenue share.

## Check the result

Keep fees, refunds and tax separate from this total. The current Bits formula is a fixed USD assumption; selecting another currency does not perform an exchange conversion.

## Try a comparison

Reduce the hypothetical net subscription amount from 2.00 to 1.50 while keeping the other inputs fixed. Subscription revenue falls to 150 and the model total to 210. This shows why actual net receipts matter more than a nominal subscriber count.

## What this does not establish

Monthly revenue is not necessarily cash paid that month. Provider payout thresholds, timing and adjustments can change the amount received.
