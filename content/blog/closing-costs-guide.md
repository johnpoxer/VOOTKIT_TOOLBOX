Keep completion fees separate from the deposit when planning cash needed to buy.

Use the [Closing Costs Estimator](/tools/realestate/closing-costs/) alongside this illustrative example.

## A practical example

On an illustrative price of 200,000, a 1% component is 2,000 and a 0.5% component is 1,000. Together they require 3,000 beyond any deposit. If your quote includes an additional fixed legal fee, record it separately rather than hiding it inside a percentage that no longer matches the quote.

## Check the result

Compare the calculated components with a dated lender or conveyancer estimate. Label taxes, lender charges and third-party fees so that a revised quote can be reconciled line by line.

## Try a comparison

Keep the hypothetical component percentages fixed and reduce the purchase price from 200,000 to 180,000. The percentage-based total becomes 2,700. A fixed legal fee would not fall in the same way, so keep that distinction in your budget.

## What this does not establish

The calculator does not identify the tax rules for your location or entitlement to exemptions. Use local official rates and binding quotes for completion planning.
