## How to interpret the output

To remove included tax, divide gross by one plus the tax rate. Registration, exemptions, place-of-supply and filing rules sit outside the arithmetic.

Read this vat / gst calculator output as evidence for the decision to move accurately between net, tax and tax-inclusive totals.
