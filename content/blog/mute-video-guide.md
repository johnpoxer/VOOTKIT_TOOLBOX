Verify a silent export independently of your device’s mute button.

Use the [Mute / Strip Audio](/tools/video/mute-video/) alongside this illustrative example.

## A practical example

If you need a silent product demonstration, export the clip with its audio removed. Turn playback volume back up and play the downloaded result in a second player. A silent browser preview while the device itself is muted proves nothing about whether the file still contains an audio track.

## Check the result

Check the start and end of the exported clip and confirm its picture still plays. Preserve the original if you may later need speech or a separate music track.

## Try a comparison

Inspect whether the original has speech you may later need for captions or editing. Save a silent copy under a distinct name rather than overwriting that source. A later request for the original soundtrack cannot be fulfilled from a genuinely silent output.

## What this does not establish

Removing sound does not remove rights in the footage or guarantee acceptance by a platform. Processing still needs time to read and save a large file.
