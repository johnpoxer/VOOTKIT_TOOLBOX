Test a progressive-band calculation with a small, clearly labelled fictional schedule.

Use the [Income Tax Estimator](/tools/tax/income-tax-estimator/) alongside this illustrative example.

## A practical example

Suppose income is 30,000, an allowance is 5,000, the first band ends at 20,000 at 10%, and the next band is 20%. The first taxable slice is 15,000, producing 1,500 tax; the next 10,000 produces 2,000. Total model tax is 3,500, or about 11.67% of gross income.

## Check the result

Use increasing band endpoints and confirm whether the official schedule expresses thresholds before or after allowances. Enter taxes already paid only after establishing the liability calculation.

## Try a comparison

In the fictional schedule, increase income from 30,000 to 31,000 while remaining in the second band. Tax rises by 200, not by 20% of all income. That is the distinction between tax on an additional slice and an effective overall rate.

## What this does not establish

This three-band model is not a current national tax return. Real systems may include more bands, credits, deductions and separate local taxes.
