Evaluate extra payments by comparing complete repayment schedules.

Use the [Mortgage Payoff](/tools/realestate/mortgage-payoff/) alongside this illustrative example.

## A practical example

Start with the same outstanding balance, rate and remaining term in two scenarios. Leave extra payment at zero in the first; add a manageable hypothetical extra amount in the second. Compare the payoff date and total interest, then check how much additional cash the second scenario requires each month.

## Check the result

Verify that your lender applies extras to principal and whether an early-repayment charge applies. A model’s saved interest can be offset by fees or a different allocation of payments.

## Try a comparison

Compare a regular monthly extra payment with a scenario containing no extras, using identical starting assumptions. Record both the monthly commitment and the projected saved interest. A payoff comparison is meaningful only if the payment pattern is one you could actually sustain.

## What this does not establish

A fixed-rate projection is not a forecast of changing rates. Keep emergency cash and other commitments in your own budget rather than assuming earlier payoff is always the best choice.
