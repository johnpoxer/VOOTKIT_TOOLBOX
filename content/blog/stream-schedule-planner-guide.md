Publish a schedule that explains the time zone and distinguishes a promise from an occasional session.

Use the [Stream Schedule Planner](/tools/video/stream-schedule-planner/) alongside this illustrative example.

## A practical example

For two regular sessions, label each day, starting time, time zone and activity. Write optional extra streams as optional rather than giving them the same prominence as committed slots. The resulting image should remain readable when shown in a small channel panel.

## Check the result

Check that every day matches your intended week and preview the graphic on a phone. Around daylight-saving changes, confirm the audience’s local time through a timezone converter instead of assuming the offset is permanent.

## Try a comparison

Keep a copy of the current graphic with its publication date. When a slot changes, replace copies on each channel where it appears rather than editing just one. A consistent schedule is a maintenance task as well as a design task.

## What this does not establish

A schedule graphic does not notify viewers or update itself when plans change. Replace published copies and announce changes through your own channel.
