Consistency beats frequency in streaming, and it is not close. A channel that goes live at the same times every week builds an audience that plans around it; one that streams more hours at random times does not.

## What the Stream Schedule Planner does

Lays out a weekly streaming schedule you can publish on your channel and social accounts, so viewers know when to show up.

The point is the commitment, not the graphic. A published schedule you keep is worth more than a longer one you miss.

Open the [Stream Schedule Planner](/tools/video/stream-schedule-planner/) and follow the settings and checks below.

## Planning guidance

| Setting | What it means |
|---|---|
| Output | A weekly schedule graphic |
| Publish on | Channel panels, social bios, offline banner |
| Time zones | State one explicitly — see the tip |
| Realistic frequency | Three kept slots beat five missed ones |
| Session length | Two to four hours is typical |
| Consistency | Matters more than total hours |
| Privacy | Generated in your browser |

## How to use it

1. Choose slots you can keep on a bad week, not a good one.
2. Name the time zone explicitly.
3. Publish it on your channel panels and social profiles.
4. Change it deliberately rather than drifting.

## A practical quality check

Always write the time zone, and prefer UTC alongside your local time. "8pm" is meaningless to an international audience, and daylight saving shifts on different dates in different countries — so a schedule that was correct in June is wrong in November for half your viewers. The Timezone Converter is the fastest way to check what your slot looks like elsewhere.

## Common questions

### How many days a week should I stream?

As many as you can sustain indefinitely, which is usually fewer than you would like. Three reliable slots build a bigger habit than five that come and go, because viewers learn a schedule they can trust.

### How long should each stream be?

Two to four hours suits most channels — long enough for people to find you mid-stream, short enough to stay energetic. Discovery favours longer sessions somewhat, but not at the cost of quality.

### What about time zones?

State yours explicitly and give a UTC equivalent. Daylight saving changes on different dates in different regions, which silently breaks a schedule that was correct a month ago.

### What if I need to miss a stream?

Say so in advance on the same channels where the schedule lives. Missing quietly costs you more trust than missing with notice — viewers forgive changes, not disappearances.
