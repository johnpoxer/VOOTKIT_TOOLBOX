Packing badly is not usually about forgetting something exotic. It is forgetting the charger, the adapter or the medication — the small items that are annoying at best and trip-ruining at worst.

## What the Packing List Generator does

Builds a checklist you can work through and tick off, so the same forgotten item does not repeat trip after trip.

The value is in reusing and improving it: a list that gets one item added every time you forget something becomes genuinely reliable after three or four trips.

Open the [Packing List Generator](/tools/travel/packing-list/) and follow the settings and checks below.

## How to use it

| Setting | What it means |
|---|---|
| Format | A checklist you tick off |
| Best built | Once, then reused and refined |
| Group by | Category — documents, electronics, clothing, health |
| Highest-value items | Chargers, adapters, medication, documents |
| Add to it | Every time you forget something |
| Runs | On your device |
| Uploaded anywhere | No |
| Pair with | A calendar reminder the day before |

## How to use it

1. Group by category rather than listing randomly.
2. Put documents, medication and chargers at the top — those are the ones that matter.
3. Add anything you forgot last time before you start packing.

## Practical advice

Pack the irreplaceable things first, not the obvious ones. Passport, medication, chargers and adapters are the items that either cannot be bought at the destination or cost far more there — while socks and a toothbrush are available everywhere. Most packing anxiety is spent on the replaceable half of the list.

## Common questions

### What gets forgotten most?

Chargers and adapters, consistently — because they are in use right up until you leave. Medication, travel documents and anything charging in another room are the next most common.

### Should I make a new list each trip?

No — reuse one and refine it. A list that gains an item every time you forget something becomes reliable quickly, whereas starting fresh repeats the same omissions.

### How should I organise it?

By category: documents, electronics, clothing, toiletries, health. It matches how you actually pack and makes a gap obvious in a way an alphabetical list does not.

### Is my list saved?

It lives in your browser session. Copy it out or print it if you want it for the next trip — that copy is what turns it into a reusable list rather than a one-off.
