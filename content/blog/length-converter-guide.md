Convert a measurement with a unit label that survives copying into another document.

Use the [Length Converter](/tools/everyday/length-converter/) alongside this illustrative example.

## A practical example

A board measuring 2.4 m is 240 cm or approximately 7.874 ft. Feet written as a decimal are not feet and inches: 7.874 ft means seven feet plus about 10.49 inches, not seven feet eight inches.

## Check the result

Keep precision until the final measurement, then round to the tolerance of the job. A tape measure marked in millimetres does not justify treating a converted result as accurate to a fraction of a micrometre.

## Try a comparison

Convert the result back to the starting unit before rounding for the job. A close round trip confirms the unit choice but does not prove the original measurement was accurate. Keep the measured precision rather than adding digits simply because the converter displays them.

## What this does not establish

Unit conversion does not add allowance for cuts or tolerances. Retain the original measured value alongside the converted one.
