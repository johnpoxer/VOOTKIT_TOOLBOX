Compare salaries on a consistent paid-time basis rather than assuming every month has four weeks.

Use the [Salary Converter](/tools/tax/salary-converter/) alongside this illustrative example.

## A practical example

At 25 per hour, forty hours per week and fifty-two paid weeks, annual gross is 52,000. Dividing by twelve gives about 4,333.33 per month. Multiplying weekly pay by four gives 4,000 and omits the equivalent of four weeks over the year.

## Check the result

Change the weeks assumption for unpaid leave or gaps in contracting. Record bonuses and benefits outside the base conversion so an hourly offer and a salaried offer can be compared fairly.

## Try a comparison

Set paid weeks to forty-eight instead of fifty-two while keeping 25 per hour and forty hours per week. Annual gross becomes 48,000 and monthly average 4,000. The comparison exposes unpaid weeks that an apparently attractive hourly headline may conceal.

## What this does not establish

Gross conversion does not estimate tax or establish working-time entitlement. Use the agreed hours and paid weeks from the actual offer.
