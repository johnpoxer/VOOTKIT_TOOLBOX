Use a countdown as a visible reference rather than an unattended notification service.

Use the [Event Countdown](/tools/everyday/countdown/) alongside this illustrative example.

## A practical example

For a local event starting at 18:00, enter its full date and local time. At 17:30 on that date the expected remaining interval is 30 minutes. If you share the event internationally, write the source time zone alongside the event; another person entering 18:00 locally may target a different instant.

## Check the result

Confirm the device clock and target date. A past target may show elapsed time instead of remaining time, which is useful for a time-since display but confusing for an upcoming event.

## Try a comparison

Choose a target later today and another tomorrow at the same clock time. Check the displayed calendar date before comparing the intervals. A countdown that appears many hours wrong often has the right time attached to the wrong day.

## What this does not establish

Closing the tab does not schedule an alert. Use a calendar or reminder application when you need a notification while the page is closed.
