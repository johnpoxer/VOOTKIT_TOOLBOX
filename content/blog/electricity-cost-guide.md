Turn appliance use into an energy estimate you can compare with a bill.

Use the [Electricity Cost Calculator](/tools/everyday/electricity-cost/) alongside this illustrative example.

## A practical example

A 1,000 W appliance used for two hours consumes 2 kWh if it draws that power continuously. At an illustrative rate of 0.20 per kWh, that costs 0.40 per day and 12.00 over 30 days. A device that cycles on and off will not necessarily consume its rated maximum for the full period.

## Check the result

Prefer measured kWh from a suitable energy monitor when available. Compare the same number of days and the energy charge from your actual tariff rather than mixing weekly use with a monthly bill.

## Try a comparison

Keep wattage and tariff fixed, but reduce use from two hours to one hour per day. Estimated consumption and energy cost halve. Reducing a seldom-used low-power device can save less than a small reduction in a continuously used high-power one.

## What this does not establish

Standing charges and changing tariff periods are separate. This estimate does not describe the full bill or justify electrical modifications.
