Proposal Generator is designed to help you connect a client problem to a defined approach, deliverables, timetable and price. The useful result is not merely a generated file or number: it is an output whose assumptions another person can understand and verify.

A business tool is most useful when its result can be traced back to a decision. Save the assumptions beside the output, use consistent names and dates, and make it obvious which details came from you rather than from the template. In this guide, that discipline is applied specifically to Proposal Generator and the job of helping you connect a client problem to a defined approach, deliverables, timetable and price.

For Proposal Generator, uncertainty in **client problem** or **desired outcome** should be visible. Record a range or source rather than quietly choosing a favourable value, so this working result cannot be mistaken for a confirmed one.

## Step-by-step workflow

1. Open the [Proposal Generator](/tools/business/proposal-generator/) and state the main purpose of the output before entering details.
2. Add the prepared client problem, desired outcome, approach details in a consistent order. Keep names, dates, currencies and units aligned.
3. Generate the first proposal generator result, then compare its most important output with the original source.
4. Change **client problem** once to see whether the conclusion is stable or needs a second scenario.
5. Read the result from the recipient's perspective and remove placeholders, unsupported claims or fields that do not apply.
6. Save only the reviewed version together with the assumptions that explain how it was produced.

## Worked example

A consultant proposes a four-week analytics audit. The proposal separates discovery, implementation and reporting, then gives one measurable outcome for each phase.

This Proposal Generator example is deliberately small enough to audit. Add real-world complexity one item at a time and preserve a baseline; otherwise a change in **client problem** can be confused with a change in **next step**.

## What can change the result

The answer is especially sensitive to **client problem** and **desired outcome**. Test both before relying on it. The final input—**next step**—may look secondary, but it can change how another person interprets the output even if the headline figure or layout stays the same.

## How to interpret the output

A proposal explains why the work is worth doing. The contract later turns the accepted plan into binding terms.

Read this proposal generator output as evidence for the decision to connect a client problem to a defined approach, deliverables, timetable and price. Check whether it answers that purpose, whether its important statements trace to a source, and whether a reviewer can see what is included and excluded without a separate explanation.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- **Writing only about your company.** Correct it before the output becomes a shared record.
- **Promising results you cannot control.** Correct it before the output becomes a shared record.
- **Hiding exclusions.** Correct it before the output becomes a shared record.
- **Presenting a price without deliverables.** Correct it before the output becomes a shared record.

With Proposal Generator, clean formatting improves comprehension but does not validate **client problem** or the final conclusion. Resolve the listed mistakes before this output becomes a client document, published page or permanent record.

## Quality-control checklist

- Verify the Proposal Generator result independently against its most important source.
- Confirm **client problem**, **desired outcome**, dates, currencies and units.
- Remove example text and every field that does not apply to this workflow.
- Check the exported result on a phone as well as a desktop.
- Test each link, QR code or call to action in the final version.
- Keep a dated source copy so later edits cannot silently replace the approved result.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I use the first Proposal Generator result immediately?

Use it as a draft. Recheck **client problem**, test the important calculation, document or link, and expose assumptions a recipient could not otherwise see. High-stakes uses may require a qualified professional.

### Does Vootkit verify my client problem?

No. Proposal Generator applies the structure or calculation described on its page. Accuracy depends on the supplied inputs and whether this model fits the real situation.

### What should I save with the final output?

Keep the date, version, client problem, desired outcome, important assumptions and source records. That audit trail helps stop an old proposal generator result being reused after the facts change.
