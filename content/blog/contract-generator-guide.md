## Step-by-step workflow

1. Open the [Contract Generator](/tools/business/contract-generator/) and state the main purpose of the output before entering details.
2. Add the prepared legal names, scope, deliverables details in a consistent order. Keep names, dates, currencies and units aligned.
3. Generate the first contract generator result, then compare its most important output with the original source.
4. Change **legal names** once to see whether the conclusion is stable or needs a second scenario.
5. Read the result from the recipient's perspective and remove placeholders, unsupported claims or fields that do not apply.
6. Save only the reviewed version together with the assumptions that explain how it was produced.

## Worked example

A photographer agrees to one shoot, 30 edited images and delivery within ten business days. The agreement defines the deposit, cancellation window, usage rights and rescheduling rule.

This Contract Generator example is deliberately small enough to audit. Add real-world complexity one item at a time and preserve a baseline; otherwise a change in **legal names** can be confused with a change in **governing law**.

## Quality-control checklist

- Verify the Contract Generator result independently against its most important source.
- Confirm **legal names**, **scope**, dates, currencies and units.
- Remove example text and every field that does not apply to this workflow.
- Check the exported result on a phone as well as a desktop.
- Test each link, QR code or call to action in the final version.
- Keep a dated source copy so later edits cannot silently replace the approved result.

### Can I use the first Contract Generator result immediately?

Use it as a draft. Recheck **legal names**, test the important calculation, document or link, and expose assumptions a recipient could not otherwise see. High-stakes uses may require a qualified professional.

### Does Vootkit verify my legal names?

No. Contract Generator applies the structure or calculation described on its page. Accuracy depends on the supplied inputs and whether this model fits the real situation.

### What should I save with the final output?

Keep the date, version, legal names, scope, important assumptions and source records. That audit trail helps stop an old contract generator result being reused after the facts change.
