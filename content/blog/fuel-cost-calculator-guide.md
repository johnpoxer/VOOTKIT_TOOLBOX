Keep distance, economy and fuel-price units consistent when budgeting a journey.

Use the [Fuel Cost Calculator](/tools/travel/fuel-cost-calculator/) alongside this illustrative example.

## A practical example

For a 300 km trip at 6 L/100 km, estimated fuel use is 18 L. At an illustrative price of 1.50 per litre, fuel cost is 27.00. A return trip covering the same distance doubles that to 54.00 before changes in traffic or load.

## Check the result

Compare the calculation with your own recent consumption rather than relying only on a brochure figure. If using MPG, check whether the source means US or imperial gallons and match the fuel-price unit.

## Try a comparison

Increase the example economy from 6 to 7 L/100 km while keeping distance and price fixed. Fuel use becomes 21 L and cost 31.50. A larger L/100 km value means more fuel consumed, even though larger MPG values mean better economy.

## What this does not establish

Tolls, parking and vehicle wear are not fuel costs. Add them separately when comparing a drive with another travel option.
