Compare equivalent homes and change the holding period before drawing a conclusion.

Use the [Rent vs Buy](/tools/realestate/rent-vs-buy/) alongside this illustrative example.

## A practical example

Run the same purchase and rental assumptions for a short stay and for a longer stay. Upfront buying costs have much less time to be spread out in the short scenario. Keep property quality and location comparable so a cheaper rent does not merely represent a different home.

## Check the result

Record assumptions for maintenance, taxes, rent growth and property appreciation. Repeat with lower appreciation instead of treating an optimistic property-value increase as guaranteed.

## Try a comparison

Run a scenario with an unexpected maintenance expense while keeping the rental assumption unchanged. Observe whether the relative outcome changes over your intended stay. A decision that depends on every optimistic assumption deserves closer examination of the actual local costs.

## What this does not establish

The comparison cannot price flexibility, job changes or security of tenure. Its result is not investment advice and depends heavily on assumptions you supply.
