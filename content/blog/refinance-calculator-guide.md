Compare the cost of refinancing with the period you expect to retain the loan.

Use the [Refinance Break-Even](/tools/realestate/refinance-calculator/) alongside this illustrative example.

## A practical example

If an illustrative refinance costs 3,000 and reduces the monthly payment by 100, simple fee break-even is thirty months. Selling after twelve months would not recover those fees through payment savings alone. Extending the repayment term may also lower the payment while increasing total interest.

## Check the result

Use the outstanding balance and remaining term, not the original loan amount. Compare total repaid and any early-repayment charge alongside the new payment.

## Try a comparison

Increase illustrative refinancing costs from 3,000 to 4,000 while preserving a 100 monthly saving. Simple fee break-even moves from thirty to forty months. This isolates the cost assumption; changing the term at the same time would also affect the repayment comparison.

## What this does not establish

A break-even figure is a model, not a recommendation or lender quote. Rate changes, fees and differences in loan terms can alter the comparison.
