Use laps to compare stages of the same activity rather than relying on one total.

Use the [Stopwatch](/tools/everyday/stopwatch/) alongside this illustrative example.

## A practical example

For a three-stage rehearsal, record a lap after the introduction, demonstration and questions. A total of six minutes does not reveal whether the introduction used four minutes; the splits do. Copy the lap record before resetting so the next rehearsal can be compared.

## Check the result

Use the same start and end cues each time. Human reaction time and device input delay can be larger than the hundredths shown on screen.

## Try a comparison

Compare cumulative lap times with individual stage durations. If a lap marks two minutes and the next marks five minutes, the second stage lasted three minutes. Do not interpret the five-minute cumulative mark as five additional minutes.

## What this does not establish

A browser stopwatch is not calibrated sporting or laboratory equipment. Keep an external record if the result matters after the tab is closed.
