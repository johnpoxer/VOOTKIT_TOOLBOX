Choose a unit category and convention before entering a measurement.

Use the [Unit Converter](/tools/everyday/unit-converter/) alongside this illustrative example.

## A practical example

A recipe calling for 250 mL can be expressed as 0.25 L. Converting that volume into cups requires choosing the cup convention; converting it into grams additionally requires the ingredient’s density, which a simple unit factor cannot supply. Water and flour do not have the same mass for the same volume.

## Check the result

Keep the original unit and category in your notes. Check whether gallons, cups or tons are US, imperial or metric before using a converted value.

## Try a comparison

Convert one known reference value before a complicated quantity. For example, one metre should become one hundred centimetres. That quick check helps confirm the selected category and direction before you copy a larger result into a document.

## What this does not establish

This tool converts supported units; it does not infer material properties or repair an ambiguous source measurement.
