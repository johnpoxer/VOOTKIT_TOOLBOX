Resize channel artwork without assuming that every part of a banner remains visible on every device.

Use the [Stream Asset Sizer](/tools/video/stream-asset-sizer/) alongside this illustrative example.

## A practical example

Place a title near the centre of the source artwork and leave decorative details near the edges. After resizing, preview the upload in the destination’s desktop and phone layouts. A full-size image can meet pixel dimensions while still losing its title to a narrower crop.

## Check the result

Check the platform’s current dimensions, safe area and file-size limit. Retain the source so you can adjust composition instead of repeatedly enlarging an already resized export.

## Try a comparison

Compare a crop that preserves the centre title with one that fills every edge. Decide which information must survive before choosing a composition. Meeting a pixel count is useful only if the resulting artwork still communicates on the destination’s displayed crop.

## What this does not establish

Preset dimensions can change and resizing is not an approval check. Confirm the requirements for the exact asset type at the time of publishing.
