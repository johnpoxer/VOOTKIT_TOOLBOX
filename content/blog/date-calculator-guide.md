Make the counting rule explicit when calculating a period between two dates.

Use the [Date Calculator](/tools/everyday/date-calculator/) alongside this illustrative example.

## A practical example

From 1 March 2026 to 8 March 2026 there are seven elapsed calendar days. Counting every date on a checklist, including both endpoints, gives eight dates instead. Those are different questions, so write down which one your deadline or report requires before interpreting the answer.

## Check the result

Use the days result for an exact interval; month and year summaries may be approximate. When adding months near month-end, inspect the returned calendar date rather than assuming it stays on the last day of the destination month.

## Try a comparison

Repeat the date difference with a pair spanning a weekend. The elapsed days still include Saturday and Sunday. If the task instead counts working days, make a separate calendar checklist with local holidays rather than silently subtracting every weekend.

## What this does not establish

The tool is not a business-day or holiday-calendar service. Contracts can define their own counting and timezone rules.
