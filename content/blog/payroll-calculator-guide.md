Separate estimated take-home pay from the limited employer-cost subtotal.

Use the [Payroll Calculator](/tools/tax/payroll-calculator/) alongside this illustrative example.

## A practical example

For illustrative annual gross pay of 40,000, a flat 10% tax assumption and a 5% pension assumption deduct 4,000 and 2,000 in this calculator, leaving 34,000 annually. That is roughly 2,833.33 per month. It does not include the employer’s own contributions or every payslip deduction.

## Check the result

Compare the result with a payslip and write down each missing item. The employer-cost statistic adds only the entered employer tax percentage to gross; it is not a complete staffing budget. Use Employee Cost for a separate model including benefits and employer pension.

## Try a comparison

Add 1,200 in known annual Other deductions to the illustrative net-pay example. Annual net becomes 32,800 and the monthly estimate about 2,733.33. Do not add the same deduction again through a percentage field, or the model will subtract it twice.

## What this does not establish

Flat percentages are assumptions, not current national tax bands. Check actual payroll rules and contracts before using an estimate to promise a net salary.
