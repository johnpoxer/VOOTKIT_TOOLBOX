Calculate a fair share separately from settling who paid what.

Use the [Trip Cost Splitter](/tools/travel/trip-cost-splitter/) alongside this illustrative example.

## A practical example

For total shared costs of 300 among three people, the equal share is 100. If Alex paid 180, Bea paid 90 and Chen paid 30, Alex should receive 80, Bea owes 10 and Chen owes 70. Those balances sum to zero; the per-person calculator alone does not keep the payment ledger.

## Check the result

Agree which expenses belong in the shared total before calculating. Keep individual purchases out and convert currencies consistently before combining them.

## Try a comparison

Check the settlement balances by adding money owed and money to receive. In the example, 10 plus 70 equals Alex’s 80 credit. A mismatch usually indicates an omitted expense, an incorrect payer amount or a different split rule.

## What this does not establish

An equal split may not suit unequal participation. Record the agreed rule and resolve rounding explicitly rather than silently assigning a cent to one person.
