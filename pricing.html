Questions

Is Vootkit free to use?

Yes. The free plan gives you 5 tool runs a day across all 145 tools, and core tools and downloaders stay unlimited. You only need Pro if you want unlimited daily usage, faster processing and premium tools.

What counts toward the 5 free runs a day?

Heavier processing tasks — like converting or compressing a file. Simple browser-based tools and downloaders don't count and stay unlimited. When you reach the daily limit, you'll see a friendly prompt to upgrade; it resets the next day.

Do I need an account to use Vootkit?

No. You can use the free tier without signing up. An account only exists to sync your history and manage a subscription if you choose to upgrade.

What do I get with Pro?

Unlimited daily usage, faster and higher-resolution processing, premium and early-access tools, larger file-size limits, cloud history across your devices, and priority support.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, from your account. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for, then drop back to the (still fully usable) free tier.

How do you handle payment?

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.
