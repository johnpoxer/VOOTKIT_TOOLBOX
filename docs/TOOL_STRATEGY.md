# Which Tools To Build — Traffic vs. Revenue Research (July 2026)

The single most important finding: **your highest-traffic tools and your highest-earning tools are not the same tools.** Most tool sites over-build PDF (huge traffic, pennies per click) and under-build finance calculators (less traffic, dollars per click). The winning portfolio needs both, deliberately.

---

## 1. The revenue lens — what advertisers actually pay

Hard numbers from AdSense niche research:

| Niche | Advertiser value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Insurance** | avg CPC **~$19.87**; "auto insurance quotes", "cheap car insurance" reach **$40–$100+** | Highest-paying vertical, ~398k monthly searches |
| **Loans / mortgage / credit** | Among the highest CPCs | High customer lifetime value |
| **Legal** | Top-3 CPC vertical | |
| **Crypto** | ~**2.6× multiplier**, RPM **$20–$45** | Volatile, but rich |
| **Finance (general)** | Pays **~3× Entertainment** | |
| **SaaS / AI tools** | Durable high-CPC | |
| PDF / image / file converters | Low CPC | Volume play, not a revenue play |

Two more findings that matter:
- **US-targeted traffic commands the highest CPC and RPM.** Geography of your audience matters as much as topic.
- **Affiliate/direct sponsorship in high-CPC niches often out-earns display entirely** — "a single affiliate placement for an insurance product can be worth hundreds of AdSense impressions."
- **AI Overviews have raised the bar** for what earns an organic click — informational content gets summarised; *interactive tools* don't. Tools are AI-resistant in a way blog posts aren't.

## 2. The traffic lens — where the volume is

From the converter research: the highest-demand conversions are **PDF → Word / Excel / PowerPoint / JPG / PNG**, and Office → PDF. Image compression/resizing is the other volume monster. These are the "everybody needs this once a month" tools.

**Honest data caveat:** exact monthly search volumes are paywalled behind Ahrefs/Semrush/Keywords Everywhere and could not be pulled from open search. The *ordering* above is well-evidenced; the precise numbers are not. Before committing heavily, spend one month of an Ahrefs trial to validate volumes for your top 30 target keywords.

---

## 3. The strategy: a barbell

Build **two portfolios at once**:

**A) Volume engine (low CPC, high traffic)** — wins backlinks, brand, AdSense impressions, and returning users.
PDF suite, image suite, converters. This is also where our **"no upload" advantage** is most visceral — people are uploading *contracts and passports* to competitors.

**B) Revenue engine (lower traffic, 10–40× CPC)** — finance, insurance, loan, legal, crypto calculators.
Same build effort per tool. Dramatically different earnings per visitor.

Most competitors do A and neglect B. That's the arbitrage.

## 5. Prioritised build list

### Tier 1 — build first (revenue per visitor)
Finance calculators that target the top-CPC keywords:
1. Mortgage calculator (+ amortisation — partly exists)
2. Auto loan / car payment calculator
3. Refinance break-even calculator
4. Credit card payoff / debt snowball
5. Insurance needs estimator (life/auto coverage)
6. Retirement / 401k projection
7. Income tax estimator
8. Crypto profit & DCA calculator
9. Currency converter (exists — surface it better)
10. Net-worth / budget planner

### Tier 2 — build next (traffic + our privacy edge)
PDF suite expansion: PDF→Word, Word→PDF, PDF→Excel, PDF→JPG, JPG→PDF, PDF→PNG, compress PDF, protect/unlock PDF, page numbers, watermark, delete/extract/reorder pages, OCR, compare PDFs.
Image suite expansion: WebP/HEIC/AVIF convert, bulk resize, favicon generator, ICO, crop, rotate/flip, upscale, EXIF strip (exists), background remover (exists).

### Tier 3 — new category (fills a real gap)
SEO tools: meta tag generator + preview, robots.txt generator, sitemap generator, keyword density checker, SERP snippet preview, Open Graph previewer, schema markup generator, redirect checker, slug generator.

### Freeze
More developer utilities, more science calculators, more fun tools.

## 6. Monetisation implications for the build

- **Ad placement by page type:** finance/insurance calculator pages carry the ads (that's where the CPC is). Keep ads *out* of the working tool surface — TinyWow's #1 complaint is ads interrupting the task.
- **Affiliate slots** belong on Tier-1 finance pages (loan/insurance comparison), not on the PDF pages. Research says this can beat display outright.
- **Geo:** prioritise English/US content first for CPC, before translating.
- **AI-resistance:** lead with interactive tools over informational articles — AI Overviews eat the articles, not the tools.
