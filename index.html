Product safety

How your files are handled.

Every tool but two does its work inside the browser tab. When a tool needs the internet, the page says so.

Most tools run locally

PDF, image, video and many everyday tools process in your browser, using your device rather than a server queue.

How Vootkit works

Nothing is stored by default

Local tools do not give Vootkit a copy to keep. Preferences such as theme, recent tools and favorites live in your browser storage.

Read the privacy policy

Cookies and ads, plainly

Vootkit is free and supported by advertising on non-workspace pages. Tool pages keep advertising away from the active work area.
