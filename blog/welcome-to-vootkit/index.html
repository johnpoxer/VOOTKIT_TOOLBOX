Welcome to the Vootkit blog

July 24, 2026 · The Vootkit team

Vootkit is a growing ecosystem of fast, private tools — PDF, image, video, finance, developer utilities and more — most of which run entirely inside your browser. That means your files never leave your device, there's no queue, and results are instant.

This blog is where we'll publish practical guides, tips and product updates to help you get more out of the toolkit.

What you can expect

We're starting with hands-on how-tos for the tools people reach for most:

  • PDFs — merging, splitting, compressing and converting without uploading a thing.
  • Images — resizing, converting and optimising for the web.
  • Video — trimming, compressing and turning clips into GIFs right in the browser.
  • Money & planning — getting real answers from our finance, tax and real-estate calculators.

Why browser-based matters

Most online tools send your document to a server, which is why they need queues, daily limits and CAPTCHAs. Vootkit does the work on your own machine, so it's private by default and there's nothing for us to store.

New guides are on the way. In the meantime, the best way to learn Vootkit is to open a tool and try it.
